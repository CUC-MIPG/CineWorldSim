The Compass Vault

During a violent coastal storm, three adventurers are trapped inside the same abandoned lighthouse archive on Black Reef Island: Mara Venn, a relentless salvage diver; Ilya Trent, a cautious map restorer; and Nora Hale, a former harbor radio operator who understands the island's old signals.

The archive is packed with damp charts, rusted keys, a cracked brass compass, and a sealed vault door buried behind collapsed shelves. Mara wants to force the vault open immediately, convinced it hides the missing navigation records. Ilya insists the room itself contains clues and begins matching symbols on the wall map to the compass markings. Nora keeps hearing a repeating distress tone from an old radio set and realizes the signal matches the compass needle's strange pull.

As the storm hits harder, the three remain in the same cramped room, arguing, testing theories, and passing tools back and forth. Mara clears debris from the vault door. Ilya deciphers a route hidden in the ledger pages. Nora tunes the radio until the signal aligns with the compass. Together they discover a hidden seam in the floor leading to a flooded chamber below the archive.

Inside the chamber they uncover the true ledger and a sealed letter proving that the lighthouse keeper hid a rescue route for smugglers and shipwreck survivors. The secret is bigger than any one of them expected. The final choice belongs to all three: expose the route, or use it at once to escape the storm and save the ferryman stranded offshore.
