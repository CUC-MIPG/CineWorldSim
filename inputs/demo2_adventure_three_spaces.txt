The Ember Reef Signal

At dawn, three adventurers are separated across three different spaces in the Ember Reef archipelago.

Leona climbs the wind-slashed observatory on North Spire, where broken telescopes and storm charts line the tower walls. Darius descends into the flooded maintenance tunnels beneath the reef, where the lights flicker over old machinery and sealed bulkheads. Mira searches the archive vault beneath the harbor market, where dust-covered ledgers and shipping registers are stacked behind locked drawers.

Each of them has only one fragment of the same tide map. Leona reads a storm pattern that points to a hidden pass. Darius hears machinery moving behind a bulkhead that should be dead. Mira finds a register naming a forgotten rendezvous point called the Sunken Atrium. None of them can solve the mystery alone, and each faces a different obstacle: a collapsing stairway, rising water, and a crowd of suspicious dock workers.

As the pressure rises, they exchange signals by lantern, radio, and mirror flash. The fragments begin to fit together conceptually even before they meet physically. Leona realizes the storm route, Darius finds the underwater access gate, and Mira uncovers the final key in the harbor archives. Their separate paths all point toward the same place beneath the reef.

By nightfall, all three arrive at the Sunken Atrium through different entrances. There they align the three map fragments and reveal a submerged vault containing the Ember Compass. The compass was never a treasure map; it was a rescue instrument designed to guide ships through the reef during seasonal storms. The final scene is a joint decision made in one shared space: preserve the hidden route for secrecy, or mark it for public rescue and future travelers.
